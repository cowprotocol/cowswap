## Findings

- No actionable P0, P1, or P2 findings remain.
- The successful-completion surface is now intentionally receipt-only: solver auction rankings, the surplus/share card, injected post-trade content, and the share CTA are gated off for `FINISHED` while remaining available for cancellation-failed states. This removes the dense 1296 × 1094 post-trade section supplied in the latest source screenshot without changing failure recovery.
- The printer gained three restrained realism cues without adding UI density: a warm paper cast and slightly softened one-bit ink, a shallow contact shadow/highlight inside the exit slit, and a 1–2 px cutter kick synchronized to the final mechanical beat. All three are token-led or progressive-enhancement treatments and collapse under reduced motion.
